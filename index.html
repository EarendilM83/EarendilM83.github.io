The product in 5 lines

WhatA Duolingo-disciplined, honesty-first LMS for Georgian VET colleges — a game roadmap beside the real school (classes, grades, exams).
WhoAdults 18+ in vocational programs — Programming, Culinary, Hospitality, Management, Design. Working people, phone-first.
The loopopen → one next action → 6-minute floor → payout → tomorrow. Streaks on contracts, mistakes that pay back, leagues without shame.
Two systemsGame layer (playful, Theme A) and School layer (calm, credible) — same brand, different registers.
The lawsubmit-loss = 0 · ≤2 pushes/day · locked ≠ sealed · wounded never zeroes · no guilt copy · structured-only social.
