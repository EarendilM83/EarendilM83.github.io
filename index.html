1The product in 5 lines

What it is. 10x LMS is a vocational learning platform for Georgia's schools and training centers — one student app plus one staff back-office (D-02), mobile-first (D-03), bilingual en+ka at launch (D-08/D-19), institutional: no ads, no pay-to-win, ministry-auditable. The north star is a single number: completion — not DAU, not session length.

Who the student is. Adults 18+ in VET programs — 💻 Programming · 👨‍🍳 Culinary · 🛎️ Hospitality · 📊 Management. They work, raise kids, and learn in 6-minute gaps on mid-range Android phones. There are no minors and no parent/guardian layer anywhere in the product (D-17).

The loop. Each program is a path of units; each unit is a tower of floors (4–8 questions, ~4 minutes) ending in a boss gate (pass ≥80%). One floor a day secures the streak — completion at any score counts; the pass gates XP and league credit only (D-20/D-27). The daily habit is ~6 honest minutes.

The two systems. The game — streak 🔥, XP ⚡ (rank only, never spendable), coins ⬡ (spendable, shop sinks only), leagues, quests, chests, and Kata the mascot cat — exists for exactly one purpose: bring the student back tomorrow. The school — floors, boss gates, three exam classes (D-32), and a 50/30/20 verified certificate with public verification (D-18) — makes every minute measurably build skill.

The law. Honesty first, codified: mistakes are free and nothing ever gates an attempt (D-04); no fake urgency, no guilt copy, stakes printed = stakes paid (D-05); the streak bends but never silently zeroes (D-20); ≤2 student-facing pushes a day, brokered (D-24); social is structured-only, no DMs (D-06).

The first-month law (measured): students not bonded to a community within the first 30 days drift toward dropout — so community attachment is a product metric with a quality gate, and each engagement archetype gets an anchor mechanic: N-01 Floor 0 digital-literacy ramp (Below-Zero Beginner, ~10–15%) · N-02 prove-it floors + N-05 re-entry bridge (Confident Skipper, ~15–20%) · N-03 cohort anchor with day-1 buddy pairs (Parallel Worker, ~30–40%) · N-04 peer-mentor track (the Driven Few, 20–25 of 300 — measured).
