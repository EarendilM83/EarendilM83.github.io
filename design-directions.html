✅ Recommendation: A for the game, B for the school — C later as dark mode

The PRD already rules this (two-design-systems law): the gamified layer and the classroom layer are different emotional products. Direction A is the student-facing game core — roadmap, floors, streaks, league, Kata — because motivation is its entire job and its chunky honesty (pressable buttons, visible progress) is exactly what a working adult with 6 minutes needs. Direction B dresses the school — dashboard, grades, exams, statements, every staff surface — where calm read as credibility, and it's the cheapest to build well. Direction C is real but risky as a default: keep it as the future "dark mode" skin of Direction A (the constellation map already exists there), after a WCAG contrast pass.
