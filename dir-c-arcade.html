Direction C — Night Arcade

The app as a night sky you travel through: a constellation roadmap, glowing state, and Kata as a luminous companion. Premium game-UI energy for a coding-forward cohort — electric, immersive, never gamer-crude.

For whom

Web Dev learners who live in dark-mode tools (VS Code, terminals, Discord). The deep-space palette and mono HUD speak their dialect; the constellation map borrows the journey-map energy they already love.

Why it works

Glow is used as information, not decoration: gold = banked progress, blue = current, dim = locked, red-violet = boss. State is readable at a glance, and the streak/energy economy gets literal light.

Risk

Dark UIs invite low-contrast text and eye strain in daylight. Mitigations: body text ≥ #8FA0CC on #0B1020, glows never carry meaning alone, and this direction requires a light-mode sibling plus a WCAG contrast audit before build.
