VET Learning App · Georgia · Concept

Direction A — Playful Bold

Why this direction

Duolingo-grade game language — chunky 3D buttons, a candy-map roadmap, streaks and coins — turns repetitive vocational drills into a habit loop. Progress is always visible and every tap feels rewarding.

For whom

Adults 18+ in Georgian VET programs who study on mobile in short bursts between work and family. They need momentum and encouragement, not another corporate LMS dashboard.

Risk to watch

Playfulness can read as childish in certification contexts. Mitigation: confident adult copy, warm paper + ink navy keep it premium, and Kata the cat coaches instead of babysits.
